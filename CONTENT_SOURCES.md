## Cultural language

The site uses `hoʻokipa` in its established hospitality sense:

- University of Hawaiʻi System, “Hawaiian Word of the Week: Hoʻokipa”:
  <https://www.hawaii.edu/news/2025/12/09/hawaiian-word-of-the-week-hookipa/>
- Native Hawaiian Hospitality Association, Hoʻokipa training description:
  <https://www.nahha.com/training>

These references describe hoʻokipa as hospitality and as a relationship among
host, guest, and place. The website uses the term as a design value, not as a
claim of cultural authority.
