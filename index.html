01 · The Opportunity

The island deserves stops that belong to the journey.

Hawaiʻi Island is connected by long, remarkable drives between communities, landscapes, and coastlines. Charging can support that movement while giving drivers a place that feels useful, comfortable, and distinctly local.

  • Start with the place.
  • Design for the road.
  • Build with a long view.

The best roadside places do more than solve a practical need. They give the road a little character.

Rangeway imagines charging as one part of a thoughtful island stop, with shade, clean and comfortable places to reset, regional food and products, and useful information for the road ahead.

The idea takes cues from the Japanese michi no eki, or road station, while leaving room for each Hawaiʻi Island location to respond to its own community and landscape.

02 · Hospitality

Hospitality is the infrastructure people remember.

We think like hotel operators, not utility companies. The experience starts with a reliable charge, then extends to the details that make time on the road feel cared for.

03 · Resilient Energy

Designed to work with the island, not against it.

Where a location calls for it, solar generation and battery storage can support fast charging, improve resilience, and reduce reliance on constrained grid infrastructure.

A

Sunlight

Solar canopies and nearby generation can turn an abundant island resource into useful energy for the road.

B

Storage

Battery storage can hold energy, support dependable operations, and help a site respond to local grid conditions.

C

Charging

Fast, dependable charging is the anchor. The surrounding experience is what turns the stop into part of the journey.

04 · The Rangeway Standard

A charging stop with a point of view.

Every future location would be shaped site by site. The standard is not sameness. It is a consistent level of care.

01

Reliable by design

Charging and energy systems planned around the conditions of each location.

02

Comfort that matters

Shade, restrooms, seating, and climate-conscious spaces designed around the driver.

03

Local at every turn

Food, products, materials, and regional character that belong to the community around the stop.

04

Built for the long road

A practical, durable platform that can grow thoughtfully without announcing a network before it exists.
